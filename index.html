Build tips
Click a part, then click near another part to snap it. Side-mounted parts (boosters, fins, legs) snap to the sides — with mirror symmetry they come in pairs. Click a placed part to pick it up (with everything beyond it), right-click to delete. Mouse wheel zooms, Shift-drag or middle-drag pans. Keep the CoP below the CoM for a stable rocket.
